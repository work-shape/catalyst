# Plain language

1. Read the whole text first. Note every fact, number, date and name in it.
2. Write one idea per sentence. Split a sentence that needs a second reading.
3. Prefer the common word: "use", not "utilize"; "help", not "facilitate".
4. Name who does what. "The team checks the invoice", not "the invoice is checked".
5. Cut filler such as "it is important to note that".
6. Keep the reader's form of address and the tone of the original.
7. Check the result against your notes from step 1. Nothing may be missing or changed.

Return the rewritten text first. After it, list in one or two lines anything you could not keep, and why.
